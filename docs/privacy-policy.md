# Privacy Policy for BookBoard

Last updated: October 2026

BookBoard operates as an offline digital document reader and personal study note organizer for Android.

### 1. Data Collection & Access
* **Storage Access:** BookBoard accesses local files (`.pdf`, `.epub`, `.mobi`, `.cbz`, `.cbr`, `.txt`, `.docx`) solely to render documents selected by the user.
* **Offline Operation:** All documents, reading statistics, annotations, and study notes remain stored exclusively on your device. We do not transmit or sell personal data.

### 2. App Store Compliance
BookBoard complies with Google Play Developer Program Policies and Play Protect standards.
